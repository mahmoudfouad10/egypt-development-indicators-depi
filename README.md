# Egypt Development Indicators - Power BI (DEPI Graduation Project)

Team graduation project from the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI): a Power BI report titled "A Comprehensive Analysis of Egypt's Development Indicators".

## My contribution: the Education page

The Education page includes:

- Cards: Total Students, Avg Overall Score, Avg Attendance %, Dropout Risk %, Avg Infra Score
- Arabic, Math and Science scores by school type
- Overall score by academic year and school type
- Infrastructure score vs overall score by governorate (scatter chart)
- Dropout risk by governorate (treemap)
- Score by parent education
- Governorate and school type table
- Region, school type and academic year slicers

The rest of the report was built as a team.
